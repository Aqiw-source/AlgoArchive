# Local illustrations and navigation artwork

The three sidebar illustrations and the navigation artwork were supplied by
the maintainer. The original PNG files are retained for full-size previews.
Their WebP sidebar thumbnails preserve the full composition and aspect ratio,
add a transparent edge fade, and scale to at most 600 pixels tall. Thumbnails
use WebP quality 88; they are lossy display derivatives, not original files.
The processing script is `scripts/process-sidebar-art.py`.

The repository-root ACM-ICPC-Windows.ico supplies the native launcher and
installer icon. web/app.ico is the browser favicon served under the web root.
